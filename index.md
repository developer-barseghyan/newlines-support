# New Lines — Support

A colour-lines puzzle. Move a ball, line up five or more of the same colour, and
clear the board — across dozens of levels that grow, add colours and, later on,
put you on a clock. Free to play, on iPhone and Android.

It is listed on the App Store as **New Lines 22**.

## Contact

Questions, bugs, or feedback? Email **developer.barseghyan@gmail.com** — we
usually reply within 2 business days. If it is about a purchase, include the
platform (iPhone or Android) and roughly when you bought it.

## FAQ

**Is this a subscription?**
No. Everything in the store is a one-time purchase: coin packs, and “Remove
Ads”. Nothing renews and nothing expires.

**What are coins for?**
Undo a move, use a bomb, or open a level or a whole package early without
playing up to it. You earn coins by finishing levels — a better rating pays
more — and you can pick up a few more by watching a short video from the store.
Buying a pack is optional; every level can be reached by playing.

**I bought coins and they have not appeared.**
Close the game fully and open it again — the store sometimes confirms a purchase
a moment after you leave it. If the coins are still missing after that, email us
with the platform and the date of the purchase.

**How do I get “Remove Ads” back on a new phone?**
Open the store in the game (the coin counter or the cart button) and tap
**Restore**. Sign in with the same Apple Account or Google account you bought
with. Coin packs are used up when you buy them and are not restored — they were
already added to your balance at the time.

**Can I get a refund?**
Refunds are handled by the store, not by us. On iPhone use
[reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com); on Android use
the Google Play order history or
[support.google.com/googleplay](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637).

**Do I need an account?**
No. There is no sign-up, no login and no password. Purchases are tied to your
Apple Account or Google account by the store.

**Does my progress carry over to another device?**
On iPhone, level progress and your coin balance sync through iCloud to other
devices signed in to the same Apple Account, as long as iCloud Drive is on. On
Android, progress stays on the device it was made on.

**Why do I see ads, and how do I stop them?**
The game is free and shows a banner while you play, an occasional full-screen ad
between levels, and — only when you choose it — a video that pays out coins.
**Remove Ads** in the store turns them all off for good — including the coin
videos, so the “free coins” offers disappear from the store and the
level-complete card once you have bought it.

**I was asked whether I allow tracking / a consent form came up.**
In the European Economic Area and the UK a consent form appears before any ad is
requested; on iPhone the system also asks once whether the game may use your
advertising identifier. Either answer is fine — you will still see ads, they
will just be less tailored if you decline.

**I quit a level and lost my progress on it.**
Leaving a level mid-way does not save it — the game warns you before you go.
A level you have finished stays finished, with its stars.

**Some levels have a timer.**
On those levels you have a set number of seconds per move, shown above the
board. Run out and your turn is skipped: the next balls drop onto the board as
if you had moved.

**I got a notification about a level (iPhone).**
If you leave a level unfinished, the game sends one reminder two days later. It
is a local reminder from the app itself, not a message from a server. Turn it
off in iOS **Settings → Notifications → New Lines**.

**Can I replay the tutorial?**
Yes — open **Settings** (the gear) and tap **Tutorial**, from the menu or from
inside a level.
