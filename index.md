# Support

**New Lines: Color Match** is a color-lines puzzle. Move a ball, line up four or more of the same color (five on the later levels), and clear the board, across dozens of levels that grow, add colors and, later on, put you on a clock. Free to play on iPhone and Android.

[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1616641155) · [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.tb.norlines)

## Frequently asked questions

### Is this a subscription?

No. Everything in the store is a one-time purchase: coin packs, and **Remove Ads**. Nothing renews and nothing expires.

### What are coins for?

Undo a move, use a bomb, or open a level or a whole package early without playing up to it. You earn coins by finishing levels (a better rating pays more), and you can pick up a few more by watching a short video from the store. Buying a pack is optional; every level can be reached by playing.

### I bought coins and they have not appeared.

Close the game fully and open it again. The store sometimes confirms a purchase a moment after you leave it. If the coins are still missing after that, email me with the platform and the date of the purchase.

### How do I get Remove Ads back on a new phone?

Open the store in the game (the coin counter or the cart button) and tap **Restore**. Sign in with the same Apple Account or Google account you bought with. Coin packs are used up when you buy them and are not restored; they were already added to your balance at the time.

### Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store, not by me. On iPhone use [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com); on Android use your Google Play order history or [Google Play Help](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637).

### Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, no login and no password. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account or Google account by the store.

### Does my progress carry over to another device?

On iPhone, level progress and your coin balance sync through iCloud to other devices signed in to the same Apple Account, as long as iCloud is on for the game. On Android, progress is kept on the device; if Android's own backup to your Google account is turned on, the system may include it and bring it back when you set up a new phone.

### Why do I see ads, and how do I stop them?

The game is free and shows a banner while you play, an occasional full-screen ad between levels, and, only when you choose it, a video that pays out coins. **Remove Ads** in the store turns them all off for good, including the coin videos, so the "free coins" offers disappear from the store and the level-complete card once you have bought it.

### A consent form or a tracking question came up.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, a consent form appears before any ad is requested. On iPhone the system also asks once whether the game may use your advertising identifier. Either answer is fine: you still see ads, they are just less tailored if you decline.

To change your choice later, open **Settings** (the gear) and tap **Privacy Settings**. The button appears only in regions where the form is required.

### I quit a level and lost my progress on it.

Leaving a level mid-way does not save it; the game warns you before you go. A level you have finished stays finished, with its stars.

### Some levels have a timer.

On those levels you have a set number of seconds per move, shown above the board. Run out and your turn is skipped: the next balls drop onto the board as if you had moved.

### I got a notification about a level (iPhone).

If you leave a level unfinished, the game sends one reminder two days later. It is a local reminder from the app itself, not a message from a server. Turn it off in iOS **Settings → Notifications → New Lines**.

### Can I replay the tutorial?

Yes. Open **Settings** (the gear) and tap **Tutorial**, from the menu or from inside a level.
